Phase 01 // BBCH 00 — Dormant Seed 01

The True Black Seed.

The cycle begins inside real Allium cepa seeds—intensely black, rhomboidal-to-trigonous capsules enclosed in a wrinkled, protective seed coat (testa). Inside lies a curved cylindrical embryo coiled within lipid-rich endosperm reserves.

✦
Rugose Black Testa: Microscopic epidermal ridges lock out soil pathogens until moisture imbibition begins.
✦
High-Density Endosperm: Fuels the initial 10–14 days of subterranean radicle elongation before photosynthesis.
6 × 4 mm Seed Caliber
3.6 g 1000-Seed Wt
98.4% Genetic Purity
Phase 02 // BBCH 09–11 — Emergence 02

Loop & Flag-Leaf Stage.

Upon soil moisture absorption, the primary radicle anchors downward while the tubular cotyledon arches upward in a signature 180° "loop stage." As it straightens into the whip or flag-leaf stage, it hoists the black seed coat into the air.

✦
Epigeal Hook Mechanics: The bent cotyledon knee breaks through crusted topsoil while shielding the delicate meristem.
✦
First True Leaf: Pierces through the cotyledon sheath pore 14 to 21 days after sowing.
10–14 d Emergence Time
18–22°C Ideal Soil Temp
94.0% Field Stand
Phase 03 // BBCH 19–41 — Field Canopy 03

Hollow Tubular Canopy.

In the open field, the crop builds an erect photosynthetic architecture of bluish-green, hollow D-shaped leaves coated in protective wax. Each leaf blade directly generates one concentric storage ring inside the future onion bulb.

✦
1-to-1 Leaf-to-Ring Ratio: Vigorous vegetative leaf count determines final bulb diameter and scale thickness.
✦
Epicuticular Wax Shield: Repels water droplets and reduces fungal foliar blight and thrips feeding damage.
9–13 Active Leaves
55 cm Canopy Height
12–14 hr Photoperiod
Phase 04 // BBCH 47–49 — Bulb Swelling 04

The Mature Vegetable Bulb.

Triggered by critical daylength and temperature, sugars translocate rapidly into the leaf bases. The bulb swells into concentric fleshy tunics, the neck softens and topples ("neck fall"), and outer skins cure into lustrous papery armor.

✦
Concentric Scale Storage: Rich in fructans and organosulfur compounds that sustain second-season flowering.
✦
Vernalization Induction: Cold storage conditioning (4–10°C) primes the internal apical meristem to bolt.
85–100 mm Bulb Diameter
13.5% Brix Solids
115 Days Harvest Cycle
Phase 05 // BBCH 51–65 — Umbel Anthesis 05

Scape Bolting & Flowering.

In the reproductive phase, the vernalized bulb drives hollow, inflated floral stalks (scapes) over 1 meter skyward. The papery spathe splits at the crown, opening a spherical umbel of 200 to 1,000+ star-like white florets.

✦
Protandrous Pollination: Anthers shed pollen 2–3 days before the stigma becomes receptive, ensuring hybrid vigor.
✦
Superior Trilocular Ovary: Each floret holds a green 3-chambered ovary at its center that develops into the onion fruit.
800+ Florets / Head
6 Tepals Floral Symmetry
88.0% Outcrossing
Phase 06 // BBCH 71–89 — Onion Fruit Capsule 06

Onion Fruit & Seed Dehiscence.

Following pollination, the fertilized green ovary swells into the true botanical onion fruit—a 3-lobed globular loculicidal capsule (4–6 mm). As the fruit ripens and cures to tan parchment, its 3 chambers split lengthwise at the crown, exposing up to 6 gleaming black seeds per fruit.

✦
3-Carpel Loculicidal Capsule: Three distinct chambers (locules) each nurture 2 ovules into mature black seeds.
✦
Crown Dehiscence: Dry capsule valves split along dorsal sutures, completing the full seed-to-seed cycle.
4–6 mm Fruit Capsule Ø
6 Seeds Per 3-Lobed Fruit
100% Cycle Closed
